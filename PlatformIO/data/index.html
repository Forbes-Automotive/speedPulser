The board type comes from the GPIO3 strap, read at every power-up. Test Board stops the motor for a few seconds, steps the motor rail to 5, 7.5 and 10 V, and reads each one back on GPIO1. Voltage Control

The motor is driven with one drive value (rail volts × PWM). Above 3.0 V × Nominal PWM the PWM stays at Nominal and only the rail moves, which keeps low speeds smooth. Below that the rail stays at its 3.0 V minimum and the PWM falls to zero, so the drive reaches all the way down to a stopped needle. Running speeds come from the marks captured on the Calibration page. Untick to fall back to the fixed 9 V rail and the legacy PWM presets.

Changing these settings after calibrating moves the needle: recapture the marks.

Export / Import

There are two kinds of calibration, and each file says which it is on its "type" line:

  • Voltage cal (speedpulser-voltage-cal): the Drive Calibration marks, each a needle speed against a drive in volts (rail × PWM), plus the Nominal PWM and Maximum Rail they were captured with. Used on a voltage board with Voltage Control on.
  • PWM cal (speedpulser-pwm-cal): the Calibration Builder points, each a needle speed against a motor PWM duty on the fixed ~9 V supply. Used on legacy boards, or with Voltage Control off.

Import Text opens a .txt file, shows it here, and loads it as whichever kind it is.

Export PWM C-Array saves a paste-ready firmware preset (a motorPerformanceN[] table plus its calibrationProfiles[] line, auto-numbered after the last built-in preset) for building it in as a permanent PWM calibration.
