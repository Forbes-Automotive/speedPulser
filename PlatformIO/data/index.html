Top Speed Calibration

This board can lift the motor supply voltage as well as PWM. To set the top speed: tick Calibration Mode, then raise the Voltage Lift and/or PWM Duty until the needle sits exactly on your desired top-speed mark (Max Speed = --). Then press Capture Top Speed. Every lower speed then scales linearly from this single point.

Set the low cut-off with Min Feedback Speed on the Advanced page.
